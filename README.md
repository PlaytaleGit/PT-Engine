# PT Engine
**A next-generation visual novel creation engine.**

## What is PT Engine?

PT Engine is a next-generation visual novel creation engine developed to simplify and accelerate the game development process. Its goal is to make visual novel development more accessible, efficient, and approachable for creators, regardless of their technical experience.

PT Engine is being developed as part of Playtale Works' broader initiative to expand its game development capabilities and make creating and publishing visual novels easier for aspiring developers and established creators alike.

## Development Status

PT Engine is currently in pre-development. No stable version has been completed or released, and the engine remains under active development.

**There will be no public release until the engine has undergone thorough testing and is considered stable, reliable, and ready for production.** We prioritise quality and reliability over rushing a release.

Our intention is to ensure that PT Engine provides a dependable foundation for visual novel development before making it publicly available.

## Availability & Licensing

The final pricing and licensing model for PT Engine have not yet been determined. There is a possibility that the full engine may be offered as paid software.

However, free demo builds and test versions may be made available through this repository, allowing interested users to explore the engine, experiment with its features, and provide feedback during development.

Further details regarding pricing, licensing, and availability will be announced once development progresses.
